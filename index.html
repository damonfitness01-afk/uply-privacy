Uply Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Uply does not collect your data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers, and it is built without permission to use the internet at all, so it cannot send anything anywhere, even by mistake.

Uply is made by Aldo Damon, trading as Bits & Algorithms, Cape Town, South Africa ("we", "us"). This policy explains exactly what the app does with information. It is written to be checkable rather than reassuring.

Your camera

When an alarm rings, Uply uses your camera to count your repetitions. This is the most sensitive thing the app does, so to be precise:

  • The camera runs only while an alarm mission or a practice session is on screen. It is off at every other time.
  • Every frame is analysed on your phone, by software that runs entirely on the device. Nothing is uploaded, because the app cannot upload anything.
  • No image, video or frame is ever saved. Each frame is examined for the position of your body and discarded within a fraction of a second.
  • The body positions are not saved either. Only the result is kept: that a repetition counted.
  • You can see yourself as a stylised image or a plain silhouette instead of the live picture. That changes what is shown; in every mode the analysis works the same way and nothing is stored.

What Uply stores on your phone

All of this stays on your device, in the app's own private storage:

  • your alarms: times, days, exercise, repetitions and sound;
  • a record of each morning: date, exercise, repetitions against the target, how long you took to start and finish, and whether the alarm was completed, stopped or missed;
  • anything you choose to add, such as how a morning felt or a mood rating;
  • your settings, points, streak, awards, and the date you accepted these terms;
  • whether you have Premium, and your free Premium mornings; and
  • a technical diary of app events (for example "alarm rang", "camera started"), kept on the phone to help diagnose problems. It contains no images and leaves the phone only if you choose to export or send it.

Payments (Premium)

If you buy Premium, the purchase is made with Google Play, under Google's terms and privacy policy. Google handles your payment details; we never receive them. The app asks Google Play, on your phone, whether your Google account owns Premium, and stores only the answer. We do receive the sales reports Google gives every developer (for example country, product, price and order number); we use them only for accounting, tax and refunds.

The one way data can leave your phone without you sending it

On Android 9 and later, your alarms, morning history and settings are included in your own Google account backup if you have Android backup switched on. That happens between you and Google, not us. It is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your screen lock before upload, so neither we nor Google can read it. Camera images are never involved, because they are never saved. To stop it, turn off backup in your phone's Settings; Uply works the same either way. On Android 8 this backup is switched off.

Permissions, and why each is needed

Permission Why Uply needs it
Camera To count your repetitions during a mission or practice. Never used at any other time.
Alarms and reminders To ring at the exact minute you set, even when the phone is idle.
Full-screen notifications To show the alarm over your lock screen when it rings.
Notifications For the ringing alarm, warnings that tomorrow's alarm might not ring, and the optional evening check-in.
Run at startup To restore your alarms after the phone restarts.
Foreground service, wake lock, vibrate, audio settings To keep the alarm ringing, wake the screen and raise the alarm volume.
Google Play billing (in versions that sell Premium) To let you buy Premium through Google Play.

Uply does not request internet access, location, contacts, microphone, storage, or the list of apps on your phone.

Children

Uply is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your control over your data

Because everything is on your device, you hold it directly. In the app, under Me, Privacy and data, you can:

  • export everything Uply has stored, as a readable file you save wherever you choose; and
  • delete everything, permanently, after a confirmation.

Uninstalling the app also removes it from your phone. If Android backup is on, a copy may stay in your Google account until you remove it there.

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, US state privacy laws such as California's, and similar laws elsewhere, you have rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection. We honour them in the way described above: we hold no copy of your app data, so there is nothing for us to look up, hand over or erase for you. For anything to do with a purchase, contact us and we will help with what Google shares with us. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa or the data protection authority where you live.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your information, and we do not have it to share. There are no advertising networks, analytics providers or third-party kits that send data from the app.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use Send feedback, Uply prepares a message with your phone model, Android version, app version and a summary of recent mornings, and hands it to the app you choose (for example email). You see and can edit the whole message before sending. If you send it, we use it only to reply and improve Uply, keep it no longer than needed, and delete it on request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date and version above. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app and ask you to accept it before you continue.
