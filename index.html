Uply — Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Uply does not collect your data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers, and it is built without permission to use the internet at all — so it cannot send anything anywhere, even by mistake.

Uply is an alarm clock that you switch off by exercising in front of your phone's camera. It is made by Bits & Algorithms in Cape Town, South Africa. This policy explains exactly what the app does with information, and it is written to be checkable rather than reassuring.

Your camera

When an alarm rings, Uply uses your camera to count your repetitions. This is the most sensitive thing the app does, so to be precise about it:

  • The camera runs only while an alarm mission or a practice session is on screen. It is off at every other time.
  • Every frame is analysed on your phone, by software that runs entirely on the device. Nothing is uploaded, because the app has no ability to upload anything.
  • No image, video or frame is ever saved. Each frame is examined for the position of your body and then discarded, within a fraction of a second.
  • The body positions themselves are not saved either. Only the result is kept — that a repetition counted.
  • You can choose to see yourself as a stylised image or as a plain silhouette instead of the live camera picture. That changes what is shown on screen; in every mode the analysis works the same way and nothing is stored.

What Uply stores on your phone

All of this is kept on your device, in the app's own private storage:

  • Your alarms — times, days, which exercise, how many repetitions, which sound.
  • A record of each morning: the date, the exercise, repetitions done against the target, how long it took you to start moving and to finish, and whether the alarm was completed, stopped or missed.
  • Anything you choose to add: how a morning felt, and an optional mood rating.
  • Your settings, your points, your streak and your awards.

The one way data can leave your phone

We want to be straightforward about this, because "nothing ever leaves your device" would not be strictly true.

On Android 9 and later, Uply's alarms, morning history and settings are included in your own Google account backup, if you have Android's backup feature switched on. That is a feature of Android, between you and Google — the data goes to your Google account, not to us. It is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your screen lock before it is uploaded, so neither we nor Google can read it. Camera images are never involved, because they are never saved in the first place.

If you would rather that did not happen, turn off Back up by Google One (or Back up my data) in your phone's Settings. Uply works exactly the same either way. On Android 8 this backup is disabled entirely, because that version cannot encrypt it in the way we require.

Uply does not request internet access, location, contacts, microphone, storage, or the list of apps on your phone.

Children

Uply is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. As the app collects nothing and sends nothing, there is no children's data for us to hold.

Your control over your data

Because everything is on your device, you hold it directly. Inside the app, under Me → Privacy & data, you can:

  • Export everything Uply has stored, as a readable JSON file you save wherever you choose.
  • Delete everything — alarms, history and settings — permanently, after a confirmation.

Uninstalling the app also removes all of it from your phone. If you have Android backup switched on, a copy may remain in your Google account until you remove it there.

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act and, for users in Europe, the General Data Protection Regulation, you have rights of access, correction, deletion and portability. We can honour them only in the sense described above: we hold no copy of your information, so there is nothing for us to look up, hand over or erase on your behalf.

Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer your information to anyone, because we do not have it. There are no advertising networks, no analytics providers and no third-party software development kits that transmit data in the app.

Feedback you choose to send

The app has a Send feedback option. If you use it, Uply prepares a message containing your phone model, Android version, app version and a summary of your recent mornings, and hands it to whichever app you choose — your email or messaging app. Uply does not send it. You see the whole message first and can edit or delete any part of it before sending. No camera images are included, and none can be, as they are never saved.
